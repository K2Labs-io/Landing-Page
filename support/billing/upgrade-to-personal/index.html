Upgrade to CamGrader Personal

Account & Billing · 3 min

CamGrader Personal removes the 50-scan monthly limit and gives you unlimited student scans. Here's how to subscribe.

What's included

CamGrader Personal includes unlimited student scans per month, all sheet formats, and full access to results and export features. Answer key scans do not count against any limit on either plan.

Step 1: Tap the upgrade prompt in the app

When you're on Guest Mode, you'll see your remaining scans for the month at the top of the scan screen. Tap the upgrade prompt, or go to Settings and tap "Upgrade to Personal."

Screenshot: Scan screen showing the scan counter near the top (e.g. "38 scans remaining this month") with a small "Upgrade" link or banner below it. Or: Settings screen with an "Upgrade to Personal" row highlighted.

Step 2: You'll be taken to the CamGrader website

The app opens camgrader.com/account/create in your browser. This is where you complete the purchase. Payment is handled entirely on the website, not inside the app.

Screenshot: Browser opening to the CamGrader Personal page at camgrader.com/account/create, showing the plan name, price, and a "Continue" button or email entry field.

Step 3: Enter your email and complete payment

Enter the email address you want to use for your account and tap Continue. You'll be taken to Stripe's secure checkout to enter your payment details. Use the email address you'll sign into the app with.

Screenshot: Email entry field on the CamGrader Personal page with a Continue button. Below it, a note that payment is processed securely by Stripe.

Step 4: Stripe checkout

Complete the Stripe payment form with your card details. Once your payment is confirmed, Stripe will redirect you back to a confirmation page.

Screenshot: Stripe hosted checkout page showing the CamGrader Personal plan name, price, and payment fields (card number, expiry, CVC). A "Subscribe" button is at the bottom.

Step 5: Sign in to the app with the same email

Open CamGrader and sign in using the same email address you used at checkout. Go to Settings and tap "Sign In," then enter your email. You'll receive a one-time code. Once signed in, your account will show as active with unlimited scans.

Screenshot: CamGrader sign-in screen with an email field and a "Send Code" button. The placeholder text reads something like "your@email.com."

Step 6: Confirm your plan is active

Once signed in, go to Settings. You should see your subscription status listed as active. The scan counter will no longer appear on the scan screen.

Screenshot: Settings screen showing "CamGrader Personal" with a status of "Active" and the subscription renewal date. The scan counter that appeared in Guest Mode is no longer visible on the scan screen.

Still need help?

If your subscription isn't showing as active after signing in, contact us and we'll sort it out.
