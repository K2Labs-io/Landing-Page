Scan student sheets

Scanning · 4 min

CamGrader grades each sheet the moment it detects it. Here's how to get a clean scan every time.

Step 1: Open the test and tap Scan

From the test detail screen, tap the Scan button. The camera activates and CamGrader starts looking for a sheet.

Screenshot: Test detail screen with a clearly visible "Scan" button. The test name is shown at the top and the answer key is marked as complete.

Step 2: Place the sheet on a flat surface

Lay the answer sheet flat on a desk or table. Avoid holding it in the air. A flat surface gives you a more stable image and better detection.

Screenshot: A bubble sheet lying flat on a desk under normal classroom lighting. The sheet is not crumpled or curled at the edges.

Step 3: Hold your phone directly above the sheet

Hold the phone parallel to the sheet, not at an angle. The entire sheet should be visible in the frame. Blue boxes will appear as CamGrader locks onto the detection regions.

Screenshot: Camera viewfinder showing a bubble sheet centred in the frame. Blue detection rectangles are overlaid on the bubble region and corner anchors, indicating the sheet is detected.

Step 4: Hold steady. Grading is automatic

Once locked on, CamGrader grades the sheet without any tapping. Keep the phone still for a moment. You'll feel or hear a confirmation.

Screenshot: Camera view frozen mid-scan, with the blue detection boxes solid (not searching), indicating a successful lock just before grading completes.

Step 5: See the score and move to the next sheet

The student's score appears immediately. Tap "Scan Next" to grade the next sheet without leaving the scanner.

Screenshot: Post-scan confirmation screen showing the student name/ID and score (e.g. "Maya, 19 / 25 · 76%"). A "Scan Next" button is prominent at the bottom.

Troubleshooting: if the sheet isn't detected

If CamGrader can't lock on, check: the sheet is lying completely flat, the whole sheet is visible in frame, the room has enough light (avoid strong shadows), and you're using the correct sheet format for this test. Printed sheets that are very dark or very light can also affect detection. Try a different darkness level from the Sheets tab.

Screenshot: Split view or side-by-side showing a good scan condition (flat, well-lit, full sheet in frame) vs. a problem condition (sheet at an angle, half out of frame, or in shadow).
