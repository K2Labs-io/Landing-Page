Choose the right sheet format

Scanning · 2 min

CamGrader has three sheet sizes: 25Q, 50Q, and 100Q. You don't need an exact match for your test length. Pick any sheet size that covers your question count.

Step 1: Count your test questions

Look at your test and note how many questions it has. That number determines the minimum sheet size you need.

Screenshot: A sample paper test with 20 multiple-choice questions visible. The question count (20) is the focus.

Step 2: Pick the smallest sheet that fits

Use the 25Q sheet for tests up to 25 questions. Use 50Q for up to 50 questions. Use 100Q for up to 100 questions. You can also use a larger sheet. Extra bubbles are simply left blank.

Screenshot: Three answer sheets laid side by side: 25Q (shortest), 50Q (medium), and 100Q (longest), with a label under each showing its question capacity.

Step 3: Students leave extra bubbles blank

If you use a 25Q sheet for a 20-question test, students fill questions 1–20 and leave 21–25 blank. CamGrader only grades the questions defined in the answer key and ignores the rest.

Screenshot: A filled 25Q sheet where questions 1–20 have bubbles filled in and questions 21–25 are visibly empty. An annotation or callout highlights the unused rows.

Step 4: CamGrader grades only the key questions

After scanning, the results screen shows scores out of the number of questions in the key, not out of the total sheet capacity. A 20-question key on a 25Q sheet shows scores out of 20.

Screenshot: Student result screen showing "17 / 20" with a 25Q sheet in the background. The score denominator matches the key (20), not the sheet capacity (25).
