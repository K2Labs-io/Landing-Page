Export student results

Results & Export · 2 min

For each student, you can export a PDF that includes their scanned answer sheet thumbnail and a complete per-question breakdown. Useful for parent conferences, student feedback, or your own records.

Step 1: Open the test and tap a student

From the class results list, tap any student's row to open their individual result screen.

Screenshot: Class results list with one student row highlighted (tapped). The student's name/ID and score are visible in the row.

Step 2: Review the student's result

The student detail screen shows their score, the thumbnail of their scanned answer sheet, and a question-by-question breakdown. Verify this looks correct before exporting.

Screenshot: Student detail screen showing the student's name, total score (e.g. "17 / 25 · 68%"), a thumbnail image of their answer sheet on the left, and a per-question list on the right or below.

Step 3: Tap "Export PDF"

Tap the Export PDF button (or the share icon and choose "Export as PDF"). CamGrader generates the PDF on-device.

Screenshot: Student detail screen with the "Export PDF" button visible — either a dedicated button at the bottom of the screen or an option inside a share/export menu.

Step 4: Preview the PDF

The generated PDF includes: the student's name or ID, the test name, their overall score and percentage, a thumbnail of their scanned bubble sheet with correct/incorrect answers marked, and a numbered list of every question with their answer and the correct answer.

Screenshot: PDF preview on device showing the CamGrader header, student name and score at the top, a thumbnail of the answer sheet in the upper half, and a two-column question breakdown below (Q1: student answered B, correct was B — correct; Q2: student answered A, correct was C — incorrect).

Step 5: Save or share the PDF

Use the share sheet to save to Files, send via email, AirDrop to your computer, or print directly. The PDF is self-contained and requires no internet connection to open.

Screenshot: iOS or Android share sheet showing the generated PDF being shared. Options visible include Save to Files, Mail, AirDrop, and Print.
