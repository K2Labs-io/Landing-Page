Frequently Asked Questions

Why are some bubbles not being detected?

Make sure you're using the correct answer sheet format for your test (25-Q, 50-Q, or 100-Q). Try a lighter print darkness if bubbles are too dark, or a darker print if they're too light. Hold the sheet flat, well-lit, and parallel to the screen. Download sheet templates from the Sheets tab inside the app.

The app says "MULTIPLE" for a question — what does that mean?

This means the scanner detected more than one filled bubble for that question. Ask the student to re-check their sheet. If the sheet was clean but the error persists, try scanning at better lighting or with a lighter-darkness sheet.

How do I export grades to my gradebook?

Open the test, tap the Share / Export button, and choose "Export as CSV." The file includes each student's ID and score and can be opened in Excel, Google Sheets, or any gradebook that accepts CSV.

Can I use CamGrader without internet?

Yes — CamGrader is fully offline. All grading happens on your device. No internet is required to scan, grade, or view results. An internet connection is only used to download answer sheet templates or install app updates.
