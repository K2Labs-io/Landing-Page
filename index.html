Simple by design

Three steps. That's it.

No training required. If you can take a photo, you can use CamGrader.

1
Scan the answer key

Generate a bubble-sheet answer key in the app (or use the web tool), hold it up to your phone's camera, and CamGrader reads it instantly.

2
Scan student sheets

Students fill in their bubble sheets on paper. You scan each one — CamGrader detects the student ID and scores every question automatically.

3
Review & export

See scores, per-question breakdowns, and class statistics instantly. Export results as CSV to paste into any gradebook.

What you get

Instant optical grading

CamGrader's computer vision reads filled bubbles in real time — no internet, no cloud processing. Everything happens on your device.

  • 25-Q, 50-Q, and 100-Q sheet formats
  • Multiple darkness levels for any printer
  • Handles minor sheet misalignment automatically
📊

Real results, right away

Score summaries, per-question analytics, and class averages are ready the moment your last sheet is scanned.

  • Per-question correct/incorrect breakdown
  • Class-wide score distribution
  • CSV export for any gradebook

Private by design

Student scores, IDs, and test data are stored only on your device in a local encrypted database. Nothing is sent to our servers — ever.

CamGrader supports 25-question, 50-question, and 100-question bubble sheets (A–D format). Sheets are available as PDF and JPEG in multiple print darkness levels so they work reliably on any printer.
