Refund Policy

Effective date: June 14, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 14, 2026

CamGrader subscriptions are purchased and managed through the Apple App Store. All billing, refunds, and subscription management are handled by Apple.

Requesting a Refund

To request a refund for a CamGrader subscription, please use Apple's official refund process:

  • Visit reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Sign in with the Apple ID used to purchase the subscription
  • Find CamGrader in your purchase history and select "Request a Refund"

Apple typically processes refund requests within a few business days. Refund eligibility is determined by Apple in accordance with their policies.

Cancelling Your Subscription

You can cancel your CamGrader subscription at any time through iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → CamGrader. Your access will continue until the end of the current billing period. Cancellation does not automatically trigger a refund for the remaining period.

Questions

If you believe there has been a billing error or have a question we can help with before going through Apple's refund process, please contact us at camgrader@gmail.com and we'll do our best to help.
