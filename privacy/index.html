Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 14, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 14, 2026

CamGrader is an offline-first grading app designed for teachers. This policy explains what data the app collects, where it is stored, and how it is used. We keep things simple: your classroom data stays on your device.

1. Data You Enter

When you use CamGrader you create tests, scan answer sheets, and the app records the following on your device:

  • Test names, question counts, and answer keys you set up
  • Student ID numbers detected from scanned answer sheets
  • Per-question grading results and scores
  • Scan thumbnails saved to your device's local storage

All of this data is stored exclusively on your device using a local SQLite database. It is never transmitted to our servers, never uploaded to the cloud, and never shared with any third party. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data permanently.

2. Crash Reporting

CamGrader uses Sentry for crash reporting and performance monitoring. When the app encounters an unexpected error, Sentry automatically captures a report containing:

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • A technical stack trace describing where the error occurred

Crash reports do not include any classroom data, student IDs, test content, or scores. They are used solely to identify and fix bugs. Sentry data is processed in accordance with Sentry's Privacy Policy.

3. Student Data

CamGrader reads numeric student ID numbers from printed bubble grids on answer sheets. These IDs are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted externally. No student names, photos, or other personal information are collected by the app. As the teacher using the app, you are responsible for ensuring your use of student ID data complies with applicable regulations in your jurisdiction, including FERPA in the United States.

4. Permissions

CamGrader requests the following device permissions:

  • Camera — required to scan printed answer sheets. Camera frames are processed on-device and never transmitted externally.
  • Local file system access — required to save scan thumbnails and exported backup files to your device.

5. Data Export and Backup

CamGrader includes an optional Export feature that lets you save a backup of your tests and results as a JSON file. This file is generated on your device and shared only through your device's standard share sheet. You choose where it goes (Files, email, cloud storage, etc.). We do not receive a copy of any exported file.

6. Children's Privacy

CamGrader is intended for use by teachers and educational professionals. The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app adds new features. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
